# FUN

A collection of experimental games, weird simulations, and thoroughly unnecessary answers to ridiculous questions.

## Games

### [Emperor Gauntlet](emperor-gauntlet/)

**How many emperor penguins could you defeat in hand-to-hand combat on their turf?**

An unarmed human, an Antarctic arena, and a hostile relationship with thermodynamics. Dodge pressure cracks, avoid falling through ice, manage stamina and body temperature, and see how far your terrible decisions take you.

- **Current personal best:** 37 penguins down before freezing to death
- **Play locally:** [Instructions](emperor-gauntlet/README.md)
- **Play in browser:** Planned, not yet available
